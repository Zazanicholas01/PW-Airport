## Notes

- `dashboard-app` is intended to be reached through nginx, not directly by a host-published port.
- `docs-site` is internal to Compose and is exposed publicly through `/docs/`.
- `db-init` and `db-seed` are one-shot operational containers, not persistent services.
- `./data/logs` is mounted into the Python containers and used through `LOG_DIR=/logs`.
